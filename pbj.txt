Peanut butter and jelly

Write your answers under each question.


1. Estimate. How many steps will it take to tell someone how to make a peanut butter
   and jelly sandwich? Write the number down before you start.




2. Write the steps, one per line.




3. Read your steps as a robot would: it does exactly what each line says, and nothing
   else. Where does it get stuck? How many steps did you write, against your estimate?




4. Make your steps work for anyone's sandwich:
   a. Name the things you use, and use the names in your steps.
   b. Write down one person's order. What do you need to know before you start?
   c. Now everyone in the room wants one. How do you write all the orders down?




5. Draw it (paper, tldraw or Excalidraw):
   a. One sticky per thing that happens, in order.
   b. Draw a box around the ones that belong together. Name each box.
   c. On each box, write what it needs and what it hands back. Do you use any box twice?




6. How do you know it worked? Write three sentences that must be true when the sandwich
   is done. Not the steps: what you would check on the plate.
